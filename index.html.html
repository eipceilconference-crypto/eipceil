About the Conference

Global Tensions and Wars are speeding up Climate Change. They also deepen Biodiversity Loss, Trans-boundary Pollution and Ecosystem Degradation. These impacts call for enforceable International Frameworks, not just reactive measures.

International Environmental Law has been recognised as a branch of Law since the Stockholm Declaration of 1972. It offers the architecture for cooperation and accountability, from State Responsibility to Multilateral Environmental Agreements and the Sustainable Development Goals. As Countries work toward the NDCs set around COP 26, States, International Organisations, Civil Societies, Private parties, local communities and individuals all need to act together.

This Conference is a forum for people from different sectors of Law to align International Standards with Domestic Enforcement. It will examine Comparative Legal frameworks, Business and Environment, Development, Energy Security, Climate Change, Biodiversity, and an International Framework for Territorial Environmental concerns.

About VIT School of Law

VITSOL, part of VIT University, was established in 2014 and ranks among the leading Law Colleges in Chennai. It stresses research-driven learning and knowledge over memorisation, with Moot Courts, Debates, Conferences, Workshops, Legal Aid Programmes and Faculty Development Programmes.
